Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

1. Information We Process

Depending on enabled features, the bot may process Discord user IDs, server and channel IDs, SCUM player IDs provided during registration, virtual coin balances, reward claims, transactions, and error logs.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to operate commands, verify registrations, deliver rewards, maintain balances, prevent abuse, and provide support.

3. Storage and Security

Information may be stored by the bot operator and its hosting or database providers. Access should be restricted to authorized personnel. Never submit passwords or Discord tokens to the bot.

4. Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as needed for operation, security, record keeping, or legal obligations. Contact the server administrators to request access to or deletion of your information.

5. Sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may be processed by hosting or database providers or disclosed when legally required.

6. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when features or data practices change.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the DARK FAMILIAR SCUM TH server administrators through the official Discord server.
